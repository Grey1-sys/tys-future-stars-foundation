Color

Three layers. Components reference semantic aliases only — the raw palette carries a value but no meaning. Contrast ratios below are measured live in the browser against the surface each token is designed to sit on.

Semantic — text

Semantic — surfaces

Semantic — brand & accent

Semantic — borders & feedback

Accent rule. --color-accent (#F26522) measures 3.15:1 on white — it clears AA for large text only (24px+, or 19px+ bold) and for non-text surfaces. Any control with a white label, and any accent text at body size, must use --color-accent-strong (4.91:1). The same split applies to --color-brand-light vs --color-brand-light-strong.
