Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Where does my donation go? +

Your gift funds scholarships, mentorship, and basketball development for young student-athletes, along with community events that advocate for a future free from gun violence.

Is my gift tax-deductible? +

Yes. Ty's Future Stars Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your donation is tax-deductible and a receipt is emailed to you right away.

Who is eligible for a scholarship? +

Our scholarships support student-athletes pursuing their education and basketball aspirations. Contact us for current eligibility criteria and application dates.

How can I get involved beyond giving? +

You can volunteer, mentor, coach, partner with us as a sponsor, or start your own fundraiser. Visit our Get Involved page to sign up.
