Privacy Policy

Our full privacy policy is being prepared with counsel. This page describes what the website does today, in plain language.

The website itself collects nothing automatically. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising trackers, and no cookies set by this site.

The only third-party request the site makes is to Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used across these pages.

Where a form asks for your name or email address, that information is used solely to reply to you or to send the updates you asked for. We do not sell, rent, or share it.

Online donations are not switched on yet. When they are, payments will be handled by Stripe on their own secure pages — card details will never reach this site or our servers.

Questions in the meantime? Email hello@tysfuturestars.org and we will answer directly.
