Our mission

Empowering youth through basketball.

Ty's Future Stars Foundation empowers youth through basketball, education, mentorship, and scholarship opportunities in honor of Tykeem D'Majh Franklin. Through community support and athletic development, we help student-athletes reach their full potential both on and off the court.

What we do

Educate. Empower. Inspire. Transform.

Educate

We support academic achievement by providing scholarships and resources.

Empower

We build confidence, leadership, and life skills through sports and mentorship.

Inspire

We inspire youth to dream big and create better futures for themselves.

Transform

We transform pain into purpose and communities through love and legacy.

His story

Out of unimaginable loss came extraordinary purpose.

Ty was a son, a brother, an uncle, a loyal friend, and a gifted athlete whose life reflected determination, compassion, and purpose. His journey was cut short on July 16, 2020 — two days before his 24th birthday.

His passing left an immeasurable void, but it ignited a mission that ensures his name and purpose will never be forgotten.
